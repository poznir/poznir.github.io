Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is effective as of 2026-10-09

This Privacy Policy applies to the Dizzy Steps mobile application (the "Application"), operated by Nir Nahum, a sole trader registered in Israel (the "Service Provider", "I", "me"). It forms part of the Terms & Conditions.

In short

  • Your health-related records stay on your phone. They are never sent to me or to anyone else, and you can delete them at any time from the History screen.
  • The Application has no accounts, no login, and asks for no name, email, or other personal details.
  • Anonymous usage statistics are sent to Aptabase only if you agree to it in the Application. You can say no, and you can turn it off later.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google; I never see your payment details.

1. Data that stays on your device

To do its job, the Application stores the following on your device only, in its own local database:

  • The side you reported as causing spinning, what you reported feeling (spinning, its strength and onset), and completed sequences, with their dates
  • The side you chose for the repositioning flow
  • Whether and when you confirmed the safety page
  • Whether you agreed to usage statistics, and when
  • The store's signed confirmation of your in-app purchase, kept in your device's secure storage

None of this leaves your device through the Application. The Application does not sync it and does not back it up to Google; on iOS it may be included in your own iCloud device backup, which belongs to your Apple account. I have no copy of it — which also means I cannot recover it for you if you delete it. You delete it with "Delete all history" in the Application, or by uninstalling the Application.

The Application uses your phone's motion, orientation, and proximity sensors during a guided sequence to indicate head position. Sensor readings are processed on the device in real time and are not stored or transmitted.

2. Anonymous usage statistics (only with your consent)

The Application asks you once, on its home screen, whether you agree to share anonymous usage statistics. Nothing is sent before you tap "OK". If you close the question, nothing is sent, and you are asked again after 30 days. You can turn statistics on or off at any time with the "Anonymous usage statistics" line at the bottom of the home screen.

If you agree, the Application sends the following to Aptabase, an analytics service built for mobile applications (Aptabase Cloud, European Union region):

  • Which screens and steps of the Application were reached, and the fact that a sequence was started, completed, or ended early and at which step number
  • Whether the safety page was confirmed or stopped (never which item)
  • Whether the in-app unlock was offered, and what happened next: the offer closed, a purchase started, completed, cancelled, pending, or failed, or a restore attempted and what it found (never the price, the receipt, or your store account)
  • Application version, operating system and version, locale, and the country and region derived by Aptabase from the request
  • No identifier of any kind: no account, device, or advertising identifier, and no per-installation identifier. Events from one use of the Application are grouped by a temporary session number that the Application makes up and discards after an hour of inactivity

The Application never sends the side, your reported symptoms, your feelings, your safety-page answers, or anything else about your health. Aptabase receives the network address of the request in order to deliver it, as with any internet connection; it derives the country and region from it and does not store the address itself. Aptabase processes this data under its own Privacy Policy. Data of the European Union region is processed and stored only within the European Union.

Legal basis (where the GDPR or similar law applies): your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. Withdrawal does not affect data already sent. Because the statistics are anonymous, I cannot identify which events came from you, and therefore cannot retrieve or delete them individually on request. Aptabase retains analytics data for up to 5 years.

3. Purchases

If you buy the in-app unlock, the purchase is made through Apple's App Store or Google Play. The store processes your payment under its own privacy policy and terms; I do not receive your name, address, or payment details. The Application keeps the store's signed purchase confirmation in your device's secure storage, and from time to time asks the store, silently, whether the purchase is still valid — for example after a refund. It can restore the purchase through your store account. No payment details are sent to me; the fact that the unlock was offered, bought, cancelled, restored, or failed is part of the anonymous usage statistics described in section 2, and only with your consent.

4. Data I do not collect

The Application does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or any account information, and does not use advertising identifiers, advertising SDKs, or cookies. I do not send marketing communications, because I have no way to contact you.

5. Your rights

You are under no legal obligation to provide any data to the Application. Everything it stores on your device it needs only to guide you; agreeing to usage statistics is entirely voluntary, and declining changes nothing in how the Application works for you.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Because your health records are only on your device and the statistics are anonymous, you exercise most of these rights directly in the Application: delete records on the History screen and withdraw consent at the bottom of the home screen. For anything else, contact me at the address below; I will answer within the time applicable law requires.

Residents of California and of other US states with privacy laws: I do not sell or share personal information, and I do not use it for targeted advertising.

6. Children

The Application is intended for adults. It is not intended for anyone under 18, and I do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the Application and agreed to statistics, contact me and I will do what I can, bearing in mind that the statistics cannot be traced to a person.

7. Security

Your records are stored in the Application's private storage on your device, protected by your device's own security. Keep your device locked and updated. Statistics are sent to Aptabase over an encrypted connection.

8. Changes

I may update this Privacy Policy. The current version is always published at the address shown in the Application, with its effective date; material changes are also reflected in the Application's texts. Previous versions are available on request.
